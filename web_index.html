Booking Options & Policies

Explore our booking methods, à-la-carte services, security requirements, and guidelines.

Three Ways to Book

  • Venue Only: Hire approved outside vendors. Includes contracted space, tables/chairs, standard layout, and venue attendant.
  • À La Carte: Add only the venue services you need (coordination, bar, setup, kitchen access).
  • Curated Full-Service: Receive one organized proposal combining venue, coordination, bar package, and catering.

Key Services & Fees

  • Cleaning: Ranges from $100 (one side, up to 50 guests) to $300 (both sides, 151–250 guests).
  • Security: Determined by guest count and alcohol service (billed at cost plus 15% administration fee).
  • Important Notes: A 25% nonrefundable booking retainer is required. All alcohol and bar service are venue-exclusive.
